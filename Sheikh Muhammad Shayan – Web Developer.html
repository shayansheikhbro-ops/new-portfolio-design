About Shayan

Sheikh Muhammad Shayan is a web developer who lives in Baldia Town, Karachi. He learned the craft of building websites at The Catalyst Institute, and he studied in English right up to Matriculation at Dolphin Grammar School.

He is also a Hafiz-e-Quran, someone who has memorised the entire Quran. That takes years of daily revision, humility and focus, and those same habits show up in his work: he pays attention to small details, keeps going until a job is properly finished, and takes pride in doing it right.
